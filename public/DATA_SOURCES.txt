TREE OF LIFE EXPLORER - DATA SOURCES

Full cellular-life map: Open Tree of Life synthetic tree opentree16.1,
taxonomy 3.7draft3. Source identifiers, topology, labels and checksums are
recorded in /data/life/manifest.json and its versioned manifest.
Release download: https://files.opentreeoflife.org/synthesis/opentree16.1/
Birds: OpenTree 16.1 Aves API snapshot, with versioned source provenance.
Primates: earlier OpenTree snapshot; its synthesis version is unverified.

Scientific profiles: OpenTree supplies identity and topology. Rich records use
exact accepted GBIF matches for rank, common names and synonyms, and attributed
English Wikipedia extracts after Wikidata taxon checks. The other tree-wide
records intentionally contain OpenTree identity and provenance only. Versioned
source identifiers, retrieval dates and checksums are recorded under
/data/profiles/.

Guided Journeys: editorial text and step-level scientific sources are recorded
in each versioned file under /data/journeys/. Illustrations use curated,
license-checked Wikimedia Commons or GBIF/iNaturalist media. Published steps
carry creator, license and source URL; the authoring registry also records the
verified file checksum. Images are optimized locally and served from
/images/journeys/.

Layout inspired by de Vienne (2016), Lifemap: Exploring the Entire Tree of
Life. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.2001624
This is an independent implementation; no Lifemap imagery or tiles are used.
Display distances and areas do not represent evolutionary time or abundance.
