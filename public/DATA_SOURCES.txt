TREE OF LIFE EXPLORER - DATA SOURCES

Full cellular-life map: Open Tree of Life synthetic tree opentree16.1,
taxonomy 3.7draft3. Source identifiers, topology, labels and checksums are
recorded in /data/life/manifest.json and its versioned manifest.
Release download: https://files.opentreeoflife.org/synthesis/opentree16.1/
Birds: OpenTree 16.1 Aves API snapshot, with versioned source provenance.
Primates: earlier OpenTree snapshot; its synthesis version is unverified.

OpenTree et al., Open Tree of Life Synthetic Tree:
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3937741
OpenTree et al., Open Tree of Life Taxonomy:
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3937750
Project data-rights statement (CC0 where not limited by pre-existing terms):
https://tree.opentreeoflife.org/opentree/about/licenses

Layout inspired by de Vienne (2016), Lifemap: Exploring the Entire Tree of
Life. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.2001624
This is an independent implementation; no Lifemap imagery or tiles are used.
Display distances and areas do not represent evolutionary time or abundance.
